Quantitative Research · Systematic Trading · Applied Mathematics

Sean Muller

I am an undergraduate at the University of Miami, graduating in December 2026 with a B.A. in Mathematics and a B.S. in Finance & Accounting.

I study cross-sectional and relative-value behavior in U.S. equities as an empirical question: a test that can fail, and a result that has to survive point-in-time data, dependence-aware inference, a full count of the trials behind it and realistic transaction costs.

I am looking for full-time, new-graduate roles in quantitative research, quantitative trading and related systematic-investing work, starting around or after December 2026. Resume (PDF) and contact details.

Research areas

Cross-sectional and relative-value effects

Whether a move away from comparable stocks is temporary price pressure or a lasting repricing, and when the difference pays after costs.

Validation and inference

What it takes to believe a backtest, and how much a two-year sample can resolve.

Execution and capacity

Cost as a function of spread, liquidity, volatility and order size, fills that can be partial, and how much of an idea survives as size grows.
