Independent research · 2026

Systematic equity research, with the validation shown.

I direct an independent research programme on one systematic equity strategy: the data platform, the research and replay tooling, the validation standards, the execution-cost model and the live trading and monitoring stack.

Results are reported the hard way: on a universe rebuilt as of each date, with spread, market impact and partial fills charged on every order.

Status: live trading with real capital is underway. Historical performance figures on this site remain clearly labelled simulations unless explicitly identified as live evidence.

Research interests

Questions I keep returning to.

Systematic equity signals

Identifying repeatable effects in U.S. equities, testing whether they survive out of sample, and determining whether they remain attractive after realistic costs.

Validation and inference

Point-in-time data, placebo controls, family-wise error control and how much a two-year sample can actually resolve.

Execution and capacity

Spread, market impact, partial fills and how modelled cost grows with account size.
