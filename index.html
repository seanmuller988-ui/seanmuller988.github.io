Selected case studies

Data

Restating a backtest built with hindsight

An audit of my first reported backtest found that its list of tradable stocks had been read from a present-day snapshot; rebuilt as of each date, the result was restated lower.

Walk-forward testing had protected the fitted parameters, not the universe.

Read case study →

Market hypothesis

A market hypothesis of my own, tested to rejection

I expected widely watched chart triggers on heavily traded instruments to produce predictable reactions; three controls removed every tradable claim.

A count of events is not a count of observations when the instruments share a market.

Read case study →

Statistics

Selecting on a noisy proxy

A buy rule keyed to a model-based price proxy passed dose-response, period splits and placebos; the effect was measurement error shared by the selection and the price it was scored against.

The standard battery is blind to a shared error by construction.
