Case studies

Where the work was wrong, and what changed

Short post-mortems from the research record. Each starts with something I believed or relied on, the evidence that it was incomplete, and what replaced it.

Each is a completed post-mortem: the episode is closed and its outcome is recorded, and any check still open is named in the text. These describe problems and methods. Signal definitions, parameters and trading rules are omitted throughout. How the work is done is described on the About page.

Data

Restating a backtest whose stock universe was built with hindsight

My first reported backtest was walk-forward for its models, but its list of tradable stocks had been read once from a present-day snapshot. An audit I asked for found it. Rebuilding the universe as of each date, and then modeling fills, took the two-year backtest result from a superseded 4.76× to 1.87×.

#PointInTime #Survivorship #DataQuality #Validation

Execution

Replacing a flat cost assumption with a modeled fill

The first reported backtest charged a flat cost and filled every order in full. A fill model with spread, impact, delay and partial fills lowered the result, and showed that an ending multiple cannot rank cost assumptions.

Statistics

Counting the trials behind a deflated Sharpe ratio

My first reported backtest deflated its Sharpe ratio for an assumed 100 trials. A recount found about 540 runs in 62 families. The claim was restated as a range, and the next research sweep was judged once, as a family, beside placebo twins.

Research

A look-ahead audit that shared the defect it was checking for

An automated look-ahead audit had passed on every walk-forward run. It derived its dates the same way as the calendar it was checking, so it could not have failed. Every figure measured on that base was withdrawn and re-measured.

Measuring whether ranking was possible before building another ranker

After repeated failures to rank individual signals by quality, a feasibility study measured the best score any ranker could reach. It sat below what two years of data can resolve, and the line was closed as below resolution.
